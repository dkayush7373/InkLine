Privacy Policy

Inkline is built to be a calm, private notebook. We do not operate tracking services, surveillance scripts, or account databases.

Your data stays in your browser

All your tasks, preferences, categories, and completion timestamps are saved locally on your device via HTML5 LocalStorage. None of your task details are ever transmitted to or stored on an Inkline server.

External network requests

The only external network request made by this application is to Google Fonts to fetch the Fraunces and Inter typefaces. When loading these fonts, your browser connects to Google servers under Google's standard privacy policies.

Reminders and notifications

Reminders work only while Inkline is open in an active browser tab. Inkline does not operate push servers or background sync workers. When your browser is closed, notifications cannot fire. If any reminders pass while the site was closed, Inkline notifies you once when you reopen the page.

Custom audio storage

Audio files added via "Add my own music" are saved directly to your browser's IndexedDB storage. Nothing is ever uploaded to any server or external service.

Clearing browser storage

Because your tasks and preferences live solely in browser storage, clearing your browser cache, browsing data, or site data will remove your local notebook records. We provide an Export feature in Settings so you can keep JSON backups on your own hard drive anytime.
